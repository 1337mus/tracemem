# Verifier transfer corpus v1 data card

Updated: 2026-10-16

## Purpose and composition

This fictional, agent-authored corpus is a provisional development diagnostic
for a frozen verifier adapter. It probes whether the verifier preserves source
attribution and conditional meaning when the cited statement appears in a
longer conversation with incidental dialogue.

The corpus has 24 cases and 48 candidate claims. Each case pairs a supported
claim with an unsupported variant, and both cite the same exact source message
and quote. Conversations range from 4 to 8 messages, with different amounts
of case-specific side dialogue. The original evidence messages, citations,
candidate objects, and labels are unchanged.

## Split and limits

All 24 cases are provisional development data. They may be used to inspect or
adapt the verifier. There is no independent test set. The examples are
synthetic and agent-authored; no human has adjudicated them. Results do not
establish human gold labels, generalization, or product quality.
